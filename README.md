# Crowdfunding & Fundraising Resources

A curated list of helpful platforms, tips, and tools for launching a successful personal or community fundraising campaign.

## Top Crowdfunding Platforms
* **[FundMyUniverse](https://fundmyuniverse.com)** – Global crowdfunding across 49 countries with 0% platform fees and community grant opportunities.
* **GoFundMe** – Popular platform for personal, medical, and emergency fundraising.
* **Kickstarter** – All-or-nothing funding for creative, tech, and artistic projects.

## Quick Tips for a Successful Campaign
1. **Tell a clear, honest story:** Explain exactly what the funds are for and how they will make an impact.
2. **Use real photos and videos:** Campaigns with personal photos build much higher trust with donors.
3. **Share with your inner circle first:** Get your first 5–10 donations from close friends or family before sharing widely on social media so new visitors see momentum.
4. **Post regular updates:** Keep supporters informed on your progress even after they donate.
